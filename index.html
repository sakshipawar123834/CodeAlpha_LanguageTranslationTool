    ℹ️ About

    LinguaFlow is a client-side translation dashboard powered by the MyMemory Translation API, Datamuse for word suggestions, and the browser's native Web Speech API.

    All history, profile and preferences are stored locally in your browser — nothing is sent anywhere except the text you translate.
